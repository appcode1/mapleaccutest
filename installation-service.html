HVAC Installation & Service

Yorktech has been providing residential and commercial HVAC installation, maintenance, commissioning, and testing services throughout Southern Ontario for more than 10 years.

With strong engineering expertise and over a decade of hands-on HVAC experience, our team provides customers with practical, reliable, and cost-effective solutions tailored to their specific needs. We provide HVAC commissioning and testing services for a wide range of systems and applications, including:

Ventilation Systems

Exhaust and makeup air systems, demand control kitchen ventilation (DCKV) systems, ERV/HRV systems, and related ventilation equipment.

Geo-Exchange Systems

Ground-source and water-source geothermal heating and cooling systems.

Hydronic HVAC Systems

Circulation pumps, boilers, chillers, expansion tanks, control valves, balancing valves, and HVAC control systems.

Yorktech is a trusted HVAC service provider for residential and commercial projects throughout the Greater Toronto Area. Our commitment to quality workmanship, engineering expertise, and responsive customer service has helped us build strong and lasting relationships with our customers.

We offer free estimates and are committed to delivering reliable HVAC solutions that meet our customers' performance, comfort, efficiency, and budget requirements.
